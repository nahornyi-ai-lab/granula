Granula is a meeting recorder for Mac that stays on your Mac. It hears both sides of a call
without a bot joining, shows every phrase live with a translation, and leaves one clear note per
meeting. This is the first downloadable build: an alpha, built for its author's own meetings.

## What's included

- Records your microphone (**Me**) and everything the Mac plays except Granula itself (**Them**,
  the other side of the call) on separate tracks, without a bot in the call. A panel offers to
  record when Zoom, Teams, Slack, Telegram, WhatsApp, FaceTime, Discord, or a call in Chrome,
  Safari, Arc or Firefox (Google Meet included) holds the microphone; in any other browser, or any
  time, press ⌥⌘R.
- Transcription on the Mac with Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3 (FluidAudio, Core ML), including speech that
  switches languages mid-sentence.
- The **Live window**: the conversation as it is said, with a translation under every phrase,
  made by macOS's own on-device translation (macOS 26+).
- **Hints** (optional): when the other side expects your reply, a suggested answer appears next
  to the live transcript in their language, with a translation into yours. A decision model
  judges in a fraction of a second whether a reply is expected; only then Claude Haiku 5.5 writes
  it from the conversation, your notes and "About me". It needs your own
  [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai) key in **Granula → Hints…**; without a key nothing is sent.
- One Markdown note per meeting: the gist, decisions, tasks as who — what — when, open questions
  and key numbers, written by the `claude` CLI.
- Your calendar, read-only: upcoming meetings, a reminder a minute before each one, and the
  meeting's title and participants on the recording.

## Requirements

- A Mac with Apple Silicon. Intel Macs are not supported.
- macOS 14.4 or later. So far it has been tested on macOS 27 only.
- Live translation: macOS 26 or later and the language packs (Granula → Translation…).
- About 600 MB of disk for the speech model, downloaded from Hugging Face on first launch.
- For summaries: the `claude` CLI ([Claude Code](https://claude.com/claude-code)), installed with
  its native installer or Homebrew and signed in with a paid Claude plan. Without it, recording
  and transcripts still work.

## Install

Granula is **not notarized by Apple**: it is signed ad-hoc, so macOS cannot check it and asks you
to confirm once.

1. Download `Granula.dmg` below, open it and drag **Granula** to **Applications**.
2. Open Granula from Applications. macOS says *“Granula” Not Opened*: click **Done**.
3. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll to **Security**, click **Open Anyway**
   next to *“Granula” was blocked*, confirm with **Open Anyway** and your password. The button
   stays there for about an hour after step 2.

Or in Terminal, after step 1: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Granula.app`, then open
Granula. Right-click → Open no longer works for this on macOS 15 and later.

## First launch

Setup takes four steps: **Microphone** (your voice), **Call audio** (System Audio Recording, the
other side), the **speech recognition model** download, and **Calendar** (optional). macOS also
asks once for the Documents folder, where meetings are saved (`~/Documents/Granula`, or any
folder you pick in **Meetings folder…**).

## Updating

There is no auto-update yet; to hear about new versions, watch the repository (Watch → Custom →
Releases). To update, quit Granula, drag the new version to Applications over the old one, and
repeat steps 2 and 3.

Because the app is signed ad-hoc, macOS treats every update as a new app and asks again for the
microphone, call audio and calendar. If after an update the Them track is silent while System
Audio Recording looks allowed, reset Granula's permissions in Terminal and allow them again:

```
tccutil reset All ai.nahornyi.granula
```

## Privacy

Recording, transcription and translation run on your Mac. Granula itself connects to the internet
only to download the speech model from Hugging Face and, if you add an OpenRouter key for hints,
to OpenRouter: after every turn of the other side the conversation text and "About me" go to the
decision model, and for each hint also the meeting title, attendee names and your notes go to the
answer model (never the audio). The key is kept in a file only your macOS account can read
(`~/Library/Application Support/Granula`), never in the log. For the summary, the transcript text, your
notes and the meeting's title, date and calendar attendees (never the audio) go to Claude through
your own `claude` CLI. No account, no servers, no telemetry.

## Known limitations

- Alpha: built for the author's own meetings, so expect rough edges.
- Tested on macOS 27 only; macOS 14.4 to 26 are untested.
- Any other sound playing during a recording (videos, music, notifications) is recorded and
  transcribed as Them, and its text goes into the summary.
- Closing the lid stops a recording.
- Hints use your OpenRouter credits: a decision request after every turn of the other side, and
  an answer when a reply is expected.
- The log (`~/Library/Logs/Granula/granula.log`) contains calendar names and meeting titles:
  check it before you attach it to an issue.
